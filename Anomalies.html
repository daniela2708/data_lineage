Every table in scope was analysed end to end: what loads it, in what order, with which file, and what is wrong with it. Thirty one tables came out with a complete chain from source system to warehouse table. The remaining thirty two each hit one of eight distinct problems, and those problems are the substance of this report.

One thing to be clear about. Lineage traced does not mean defect free. It means we can state where the data comes from and what transforms it. Several fully traced tables carry findings of their own, and those are covered in the cross cutting section.

6, 7 and 8. Single table anomalies

OPERATOR_DIM: the table is mostly placeholder data

The real loading chain, a TLOG upsert, has been frozen since 2016. What keeps the table alive is a fact side injector, and it is not an edge case: 1,615 of 2,194 rows, 74 percent of the table, are placeholders generated by another process rather than real operator records.

RECLM_SCAN_DETAIL_F: a live feed that stopped four months ago

The integration is real and still scheduled: flow-irc-data.fex, running daily since June 2020, writing with a confirmed IRC stamp. But no new reclamation scan data has arrived since around 25 April 2026, more than four months before this analysis.

Nothing reports a failure. The staging table's own statistics still read as live, because the pipeline executes successfully every day. It simply carries nothing new.

CUSTOMER_ATTR_XREF: 124 million rows frozen since 2019

Migrating it moves seven year old data into a new platform. Whether that is wanted is a business decision, and it should be made deliberately rather than by default.

Question 3. Who owns the file based feeds?

Five tables appear to be loaded from a file rather than from a database connection. A file feed has a producer, a schedule and a format, and none of the three are documented. If the file stops arriving, nothing fails loudly, the data simply goes stale.

TableWave
DT_DIMWave 1
STORE_START_END_DATEWave 1
SALES_DAILY_BUDGET_FWave 1
CLUB_CARD_ATTRIB_DIMWave 3
LABOR_PREM_COST_FWave 5
On DT_DIM. A date dimension usually has no source system. It is generated once by a script, one row per calendar day across a long range, then extended when the range runs out. What does come from the business is the retail calendar: period boundaries, week numbering and holiday flags.

Two questions, and either answer is fine:
1. Is the calendar generated by a script, or loaded from a file?
2. Who decides the fiscal period boundaries, and how do they reach the warehouse?
